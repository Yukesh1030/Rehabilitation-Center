Clinical Specialties

Find the Support That Fits Your Journey.

Evidence-backed physical, neurological, and cognitive rehabilitation protocols delivered by specialized therapists focused on your dignity and restorative milestones.

Section 02 • Core Restorative

Physical Rehabilitation

Restoring anatomical alignment, muscular equilibrium, joint range of motion, and physical endurance.

Physical rehabilitation at STACKLY is founded on rigorous kinetic assessments. Our physical therapists utilize targeted manual therapy, therapeutic exercise prescription, and neuromuscular re-education to alleviate pain and rebuild confident movement patterns.

Intended Audience:
Post-Surgical Joint Replacement Spinal Decompression & Back Pain Osteoarthritis & Degenerative Conditions Mobility & Balance Frailty

Section 03 • Neuroplasticity

Neurological Rehabilitation

Rewiring communication between the brain, spinal pathways, and musculoskeletal systems through repetition and adaptive training.

Neurological trauma requires deliberate, highly focused repetition. Our certified neurological clinicians guide patients through bodyweight-supported gait training, vestibular balance restoration, and sensory retraining to re-establish neuro-motor firing.

Intended Audience:
Stroke / CVA Recovery Traumatic Brain Injury (TBI) Parkinson's Disease Protocols Spinal Cord Injury (SCI) Multiple Sclerosis (MS)
Book Assessment Learn More
Section 04 • Functional Living

Occupational Therapy

Empowering autonomy in the essential tasks of daily life, work routines, and personal self-care.

Our occupational therapists analyze daily barriers inside home and workplace environments. We train patients on fine motor dexterity, adaptive culinary gear, dressing aids, ergonomic workstations, and cognitive task sequencing to foster lasting self-reliance.

Intended Audience:
Upper Extremity Impairment Activities of Daily Living (ADL) Retraining Cognitive Executive Function Ergonomic Return-to-Work
Book Assessment Learn More
Section 05 • Communication & Swallowing

Speech & Language Therapy

Restoring vocal confidence, verbal expression, cognitive processing, and safe swallowing function.

Speech pathology addresses both communication and physiological swallow mechanics. Our certified pathologists conduct formal swallowing studies (FEES/MBSS coordination), speech articulation drills, vocal loudness programs (LSVT LOUD concepts), and aphasia recovery tools.

Intended Audience:
Post-Stroke Aphasia & Apraxia Dysphagia (Swallowing Disorders) Cognitive-Communication Retraining Voice Pathology & Parkinson's Speech
Book Assessment Learn More
Section 06 • Athletic Restoration

Sports Injury Rehabilitation

Science-driven athletic conditioning, kinetic chain balancing, and objective return-to-sport protocols.

Whether you are an endurance runner, recreational athlete, or competitive player, our sports physical therapists combine high-speed video motion analysis, force plate assessments, and progressive plyometrics to safeguard repaired ligaments and optimize mechanics.

Intended Audience:
ACL / Meniscus Ligament Repairs Rotator Cuff & Labral Tears Overuse Tendinopathies (Achilles/Patellar) Biomechanical Running Screenings
