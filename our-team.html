Section 05 • How We Practice

An Approach Grounded in Empathy

We understand that rehabilitation is as much emotional and psychological as it is physiological.

Our therapists take the time to listen before administering treatment. We pace sessions to your comfort, avoiding unnecessary pain while encouraging healthy neuro-muscular adaptation. You will never feel rushed or treated as a clinical number.

  • Full 60-Minute 1-on-1 Sessions: Dedicated clinician attention without overlapping appointments.
  • Trauma-Informed Practice: Sensitive pacing for individuals recovering from neurological or physical trauma.
  • Patient-Directed Milestones: Your definition of success drives our daily clinical focus.
Section 06 • Interdisciplinary Synergy

Seamless Care Coordination

Restoring human function requires coordinated communication across clinical disciplines and external medical providers.

At STACKLY, your PT, OT, and SLP conduct weekly roundtable reviews. When needed, our team corresponds directly with your orthopedic surgeon, neurologist, physiatrist, or primary care doctor to ensure unified medical documentation and post-operative safety.
