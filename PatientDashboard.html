Prescribed Home Exercise Regimen (HEP)

Supervised daily exercises to perform between in-clinic sessions
3 of 5 Completed Today
Mobility & Ankle

Ankle Dorsiflexion with Elastic Band

3 Sets × 15 Reps Twice Daily

Loop resistance band around forefoot. Pull foot gently upward toward knee, pause 2 seconds, and release slowly.

Functional Strength

Seated-to-Standing Chair Transfers

3 Sets × 10 Reps Once Daily

Cross arms across chest. Lean forward at hips, rise smoothly to full stand without using hands, then lower with control.

Balance & Stability

Tandem Stance Balance Hold

4 Sets × 30 Sec Once Daily

Position right heel directly touching left toes along kitchen counter. Maintain balance with light fingertip touch support.

Gait Mechanics

Heel-to-Toe Corridor Walking

10m × 3 Laps Afternoon

Walk along a clear hallway with wall within reach. Focus on rolling from heel strike to toe push-off with equal cadence.

Postural Reset

Diaphragmatic Breathing & Posture

5 Minutes Morning & Night

Inhale deeply expanding your lower ribcage, gently tuck your chin, and exhale slowly while relaxing neck and trapezius muscles.
